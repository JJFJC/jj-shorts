Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026.

JJ Shorts is a personal video-upload tool operated by Yongzhou (Jack) Jiang, Toronto, Canada. It is used only with accounts owned by its operator.

Data we access

When the operator authorizes JJ Shorts with Google (YouTube), TikTok or Instagram, the tool receives an access token for that account and uses it only to upload videos and read basic account and video information needed to confirm uploads.

How data is stored

Access tokens and upload records are stored locally on the operator's own computer. They are not sold, shared, or sent to third parties, and are not used for advertising.

Other users

JJ Shorts does not offer sign-ups and does not collect personal data from anyone other than its operator.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in the connected platform's security settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). Local data is deleted on request.
